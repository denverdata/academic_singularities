# Project Technical Documentation: Inertial Relativity Framework

## Core Principles
The project is built on the theory of **Inertial Relativity**, which proposes that the relationship between time, space, and matter is fundamentally mediated by **inertia**.

### The Space-Time Equivalence
The central mathematical relationship is the Inertial Scale Factor ($k_i$):
$$ \frac{t_1}{t_2} = k_i = \left(\frac{I_2}{I_1}\right)^{1/5} $$
For linear contexts, inertia ($I$) is mass ($m$). For rotational contexts, $I$ is the moment of inertia ($mr^2$).

### Mathematical Standards
- **Relativistic Classical Physics**: Use full relativistic formulas (e.g., Lorentz factor $\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}$) without approximations.
- **Dimensional Time**: Time has both linear (from translational inertia $\propto k^3$) and rotational (from rotational inertia $\propto k^5$) aspects.
- **Asymptotic Limits**: Limits like $1/x \to 0$ as $x \to \infty$ represent an approach, never a reaching of the limit. A physical system always maintains finite mass and extent.

## Prohibited Concepts (Internal Rules)
- **Cosmic Expansion**: Never cite or utilize as an explanation for redshift.
- **Big Bang (Lambda CDM)**: Not to be used as a theoretical premise, though observed redshift data is valid.
- **Singularities**: Treated as mathematical fallacies resulting from division by zero or improper limit interpretation.
